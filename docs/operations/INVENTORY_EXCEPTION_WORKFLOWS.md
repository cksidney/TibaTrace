# Inventory completion workflows

**HQ → Inventory → Exceptions & valuation** provides investigation requests for signed stock adjustments, write-offs, witnessed destruction, batch acquisition costs and remaining transfer shortages. A request does not change quantities. A different operator with inventory management authority approves or rejects it with evidence. Approval locks the relevant records and posts the effect and audit trail atomically. Retry keys preserve the original result. Reserved stock cannot be removed. Adjustment gains enter quarantined custody.

Transfer requests can be rejected while submitted or cancelled before dispatch. After dispatch, receive accepted and damaged quantities into their actual custody. Investigate remaining shortages, request closure, then have an operator independent of both the request author and dispatcher approve. Loss quantities are recorded against transfer lines. Source stock left at dispatch and is never debited a second time. Later receipts cannot reopen a closed transfer.

**HQ → Inventory → Stocktakes** offers a blind count-sheet download, validated CSV upload, individual observations, audited recounts and independent variance posting. Each row identifies the session, location, SKU and actual batch/custody lot. Duplicate or conflicting observations fail; a retry of the same observations does not duplicate counts. Full counts require every scoped stock line plus physical confirmation of empty locations. A fully verified empty session can be posted. Stock movement after an observation requires a fresh recount; reserved demand must be reconciled before posting a smaller count.

**HQ → Procurement → Quality** can record received goods in tracked quarantine before release or supplier return. Quality release transfers accepted quantities from that custody instead of creating another receipt. Partial release posts only the newly accepted quantity. Failure to post stock rolls back the corresponding quality-status update.

**HQ → Procurement → Returns & matching** records a return against the actual receipt, received batch, source custody, quantity, expected credit and reason. The aggregate claim across all returns cannot exceed delivered stock or the particular received batch. Independent approval is required. Dispatch requires procurement write and inventory management authority, consumes only unreserved physical custody and posts an immutable `RETURN_OUT`. Record the actual supplier credit-note reference and amount; explain any difference from the expected credit. A different authorized approver reconciles the credit and closes the return. This records return reconciliation evidence, not a general-ledger journal.

Valuation uses specific identification at an approved acquisition cost per base selling unit in KES. Independently released opening counts can supply that cost. For existing batches, submit a cost declaration with invoice/unit evidence and have another operator approve it. Approved costs and movement snapshots are immutable. Missing acquisition costs remain explicitly unvalued, including historical movements made before a cost was supplied. Current valuation includes physical custody and outstanding dispatched quantities once. Quarantined, damaged and expired custody is disclosed at recorded acquisition cost; accounting impairment or net realisable value is outside this operational acquisition-cost report. Published selling prices are never used as inventory cost.
