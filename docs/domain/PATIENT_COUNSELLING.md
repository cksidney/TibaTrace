# Patient Counselling

Counselling records whether counselling was required and completed, topics, warnings, administration, storage, adherence, side effects, missed-dose guidance, device demonstration, questions, language, interpreter, actor, time, and refusal reason.

Required counselling must be completed or explicitly refused before supply. The work queue moves from ready for counselling to ready for supply only after the status is recorded.

Supported topics include dosage, administration, food and drug interaction, side effects, storage, adherence, device use, controlled medicine, antimicrobial stewardship, pregnancy, and driving.
