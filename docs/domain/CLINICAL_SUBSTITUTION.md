# Clinical Substitution

Clinical substitution is separate from commercial stock substitution. `ClinicalSubstitution` records the prescribed and proposed SKU, equivalence basis, price impact, stock reason, prescriber approval, patient consent, pharmacist approval, actor, status, and reason.

`NO_SUBSTITUTION` is enforced. Therapeutic substitution can require prescriber approval; patient-consent policies require recorded consent. A proposed SKU cannot be reserved unless an approved clinical substitution exists.

Commercial availability alone never authorizes a clinical substitution. The existing medicine and commercial SKU masters remain authoritative.
