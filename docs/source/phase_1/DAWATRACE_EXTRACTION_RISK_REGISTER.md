# DawaTrace Extraction Risk Register

## Rating Model

- Likelihood: `L` unlikely, `M` plausible, `H` likely without intervention
- Impact: `M` material operational impact, `H` major financial/availability impact,
  `C` patient safety, statutory, security or unrecoverable integrity impact
- An owner and due date must be assigned before Phase 2 starts. The role below is
  the accountable function, not evidence that a person has accepted ownership.

## Highest-Priority Blockers

The Phase 2 start gate is blocked by R02, R04, R06, R08, R26, R27 and R38. They
cover migration foundations, canonical healthcare identity, authentication,
clinical-engine behavior/content and the absence of a clean source release.

The first live-tenant gate is additionally blocked until R10, R12, R14, R16,
R17, R19, R22, R23, R31 and R36 have passed measured acceptance and rollback
drills.

## Operating the Register

- Review risks at every work-package exit gate and release candidate.
- Link mitigation commits, tests, evidence manifests and signed approvals.
- An accepted risk records approver, scope, expiry and compensating control.
- A risk is closed only by evidence, not by code-complete status.
- New coupling discovered during extraction updates the inventory, classification,
  architecture decision and this register before work proceeds.
