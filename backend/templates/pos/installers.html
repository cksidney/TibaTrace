Install your approved Point of Sale till

The browser till has been retired. Use the Windows or Android application for governed dispensing, cash checkout and till reconciliation.

  1. Sign in to HQ and open Point of Sale Till Installers.
  2. Download the approved installer for your device. If Android is not release-ready, use Windows until the approved signed build is available.
  3. Install and sign in, request device activation, then have an authorised Manager approve the branch and register.
  4. Open your register and operator shift before taking payments.

This page cannot take payments, release medicines or bypass activation. Printer and scanner checks must be completed on the physical device.
